\section*{1. Specific Objectives}

The project addresses three specific objectives (from Thesis Chapter I):
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Automated Daily Price Collection \& Quality Monitoring:} Build an automated data pipeline that continuously collects and stores consumer price data every morning at 08:00 AM across 25 retail, utility, transport, and government sources in Phnom Penh, with built-in quality checks.
    \item \textbf{Price Index Compilation \& Inflation Nowcasting:} Apply standard economic formulas (geometric Jevons index) to compute daily price changes without mathematical drift, and use regularized machine learning (Ridge regression) to nowcast month-end inflation weeks before official statistics are released.
    \item \textbf{Interactive Decision-Support Dashboards:} Develop interactive dashboards in Metabase and Power BI to give government analysts and policymakers real-time visibility into daily price trends, category breakdowns, and inflation nowcasts.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.05cm}

% ── Project Methodology: The Five Pillars ─────────────────────────────────────
\section*{2. Project Methodology: The Five Core Pillars (from Thesis Chapter III)}

To turn messy internet price tags into reliable, decision-grade economic statistics, the project follows five clear methodological pillars:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Pillar 1: Multi-Source Daily Collection:} Automated computer programs visit 25 retail, transport, and government websites every morning without human intervention, collecting over 35,000 prices polite to web servers.
    \item \textbf{Pillar 2: Cleaning \& Product Tracking:} Cleans messy product text, converts US Dollar prices into Cambodian Riel, and tracks the exact same item from day to day so a small single can is never confused with a large wholesale pack.
    \item \textbf{Pillar 3: Smart Categorization:} Places every product into its official United Nations consumer category (Food, Housing, Health, Transport, etc.) using store rules and Google Gemini AI, remembering choices permanently to save cost.
    \item \textbf{Pillar 4: Fair Index Calculation:} Uses the geometric Jevons formula so temporary store sales and price bounces do not cause numbers to drift upward artificially, and balances stores so giant supermarkets do not overpower smaller shops.
    \item \textbf{Pillar 5: Real-Time Inflation Nowcasting:} Watches how fast food, fuel, and currency prices move during the first 10 to 20 days of the month to nowcast the official month-end inflation rate with shrinking uncertainty as the month ends.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The Three-Step Medallion Framework}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Bronze Layer (Raw Storage):} Secure digital vault that saves raw, untouched copies of store listings exactly as downloaded, recording timestamps and store names. No data is changed, preserving a permanent historical audit trail.
    \item \textbf{Silver Layer (Data Cleaning \& AI Sorting):} Cleaning workshop where messy text is cleaned, US Dollars are converted to Cambodian Riel, pack sizes are parsed to calculate price per kg or L, and items are sorted into official consumer categories.
    \item \textbf{Gold Layer (Final Calculations \& Nowcasting):} Math hub where daily price changes are averaged fairly, weighted by official Cambodian household spending, and used to nowcast monthly inflation for live dashboards.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Scraping Methods \& Bronze Safety Circuit Breakers}

\section*{Page 3 — Silver Layer (Data Cleaning \& AI Sorting)}

\noindent
Raw web data contains promotional text, mixed languages, and different currencies. The Silver Layer cleans this noisy information, converts currencies, tracks products consistently, and assigns every item to its proper category.

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{1. Words, Currencies \& Hidden Price Hikes (Shrinkflation)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Text Cleanup \& Khmer Numeral Translation:} Strips advertising buzzwords (``Big Sale'', ``Flash Promo'') and automatically converts Khmer numerals to standard Arabic digits (0 to 9).
    \item \textbf{Dual-Currency Normalization to Riel:} Cambodian shops quote in both USD and Riel. The pipeline ingests daily official exchange rates from the Ministry of Economy and Finance and dynamically converts all prices to Cambodian Riel (KHR).
    \item \textbf{Detecting Package Downsizing (Shrinkflation):} Manufacturers often downsize packages (e.g., shrinking a 500g box to 450g without changing the shelf price). The Silver layer parses package volumes to compute the price per standard metric unit ($P_{\text{unit}} = \text{Price in KHR} / \text{Volume in kg or L}$), immediately catching hidden price increases.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{2. Tracking the Same Product Over Time (The 5-Step Matching Ladder)}
\noindent
To calculate real price movements, the pipeline tracks identical items day after day across 50,300+ products:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Instant Barcode \& Store SKU Match (Steps 1 \& 2):} Matches by barcode or retailer SKU in under 0.1 milliseconds.
    \item \textbf{Smart Text Matching with Safety Guards (Steps 3 \& 4):} Matches similar descriptions across languages, protected by an unbreachable \textbf{Rule Firewall} that prevents false matches:
    \begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
        \item \textit{Pack Count Guard:} A 1-can soda never merges with a 24-can box (preventing an artificial 95\% price collapse).
        \item \textit{Hardware Spec Guard:} A 128GB smartphone never merges with a 256GB phone.
        \item \textit{Volume \& Size Guard:} Products with different volumes ($>10\%$ difference) or clothing sizes stay separate.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{New Product Discovery (Step 5):} Newly introduced store items receive a new identity and are tracked for the future.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Page 4 — Gold Layer (Fair Price Averaging \& Inflation Nowcasting)}

\noindent
The Gold Layer transforms clean daily price quotes into official economic indicators, aggregates categories according to national spending, and nowcasts the monthly inflation rate before the official release.

\vspace{-0.10cm}
\subsection*{1. Fair Price Averaging: Why Standard Averages Fail}
\noindent
In grocery retail, prices jump up and down because of temporary promotional discounts. Using a simple arithmetic average (called the \textbf{Carli formula}, $I_{\text{Carli}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum \frac{P_t}{P_0}$) causes a severe mathematical error:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{The Sales Bounce Distortion:} If a product costs \$10, drops to \$5 on sale ($-50\%$), and goes back to \$10 next week ($+100\%$), the real price is back to normal. However, an arithmetic average calculates $\frac{0.50 + 2.00}{2} = 1.25$---falsely reporting a $+25\%$ price increase!
    \item \textbf{The Proof:} Over a continuous 90-day test, the Carli formula accumulated an artificial upward drift of \textbf{+1.18\% per year}.
    \item \textbf{Our Solution (The Jevons Formula):} The pipeline uses the international standard unweighted geometric mean (\textbf{Jevons formula}), computed in logarithmic space: $\ln I_{e,t} = \frac{1}{n} \sum (\ln P_{i,t} - \ln P_{i,0})$. In log space, a $-50\%$ discount and a $+100\%$ recovery cancel out perfectly to zero net change, completely eliminating mathematical drift.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{2. Preventing Supermarket Dominance (Store Balancing)}
\noindent
A giant online hypermarket may list 190 varieties of rice, while a small neighborhood grocer lists only 10 varieties. If all 200 items are pooled into one big average, the hypermarket controls 95\% of the rice index. If the small store raises rice prices by 10\% while the hypermarket stays flat, an unweighted index would report only +0.48\% inflation.
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Two-Stage Store-Balanced Solution:} We first calculate the Jevons price index inside each store, and then combine the eligible stores with equal weight: $I_{\text{Balanced}} = \sqrt{I_{\text{Store A}} \times I_{\text{Store B}}}$. In the rice example, this correctly reflects a $+4.88\%$ price movement. Every retailer receives an equal vote in that product category, so catalog size never distorts the real-world market signal.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{3. National Spending Weights (CSES 2023 Basket across 76 Categories)}
\noindent
Elementary price indexes are rolled up across \textbf{76 real-world spending categories} (35 detailed food/drink groups and 41 broad groups) weighted strictly by the official \textbf{Cambodia Socio-Economic Survey (CSES 2023)}:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Division 01: Food \& Non-Alcoholic Drinks (44.78\% weight):} The largest share of Cambodian family spending, updated daily across fresh meats, fish, vegetables, and rice.
    \item \textbf{Division 04: Housing, Water, Electricity \& Gas (17.08\% weight):} Reflects steady state-regulated electricity (EDC) and municipal water (PPWSA) tariffs, combined with residential rents.
    \item \textbf{Division 07: Transport (12.18\% weight):} Captures fuel adjustments from the Ministry of Commerce and interprovincial bus fares.
    \item \textbf{All Other 9 Consumer Divisions (25.96\% combined):} Clothing, Health, Tech, Restaurants, Education, and Personal Care.
    \item \textbf{Handling Out-of-Stock Items:} If an item is temporarily missing for 1 to 7 days, its price is adjusted according to the movement of sister products in the same category, rather than pretending prices froze.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{4. Headline CPI vs. Refined Core CPI}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Headline CPI (100\% basket):} Tracks the total cost of living across all goods and services.
    \item \textbf{Refined Core CPI (52.2\% basket):} Removes volatile food and fuel prices. Over a 30-day evaluation, Headline CPI rose by \textbf{+0.28\%} (driven by fuel price adjustments), while Core CPI rose by only \textbf{+0.07\%}. This separation proves to policymakers that underlying monetary inflation remained calm and steady.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.12cm}
\subsection*{5. Machine Learning Inflation Nowcasting Model}
\noindent
Rather than waiting 30 to 60 days for official reports, the pipeline deploys a \textbf{Two-Stage Regularized Ridge Nowcaster}:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{How it Works:} The model watches high-frequency price speed across food, fuel, utility tariffs, and the USD/KHR exchange rate during the first 10 to 20 days of the month, using regularized regression to nowcast the final month-end inflation rate.
    \item \textbf{Shrinking Uncertainty:} As the month progresses from day 1 to day 30, the model replaces predictions with actual observed data. The uncertainty range naturally shrinks toward zero by month-end ($U_t \propto \sqrt{(T-t)/T}$), giving policymakers an early and dependable inflation estimate.
\end{itemize}

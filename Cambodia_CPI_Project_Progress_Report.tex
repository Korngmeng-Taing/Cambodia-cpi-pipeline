\section*{Project Scope \& Strategic Objectives}

Official Consumer Price Index (CPI) statistics in Cambodia are traditionally published with a \textbf{30 to 60-day reporting delay} because official data collection relies on periodic field surveys and paper questionnaires. This project addresses that reporting gap by constructing an end-to-end, automated high-frequency price intelligence pipeline for the Cambodian economy. 

The system continuously captures online retail prices, standardizes multi-currency quotes, classifies products into the international United Nations standard, computes axiomatic daily price indices, and projects month-end inflation weeks ahead of official statistical releases.

\vspace{0.08cm}

% ── Core Capabilities Overview ────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Core System Capabilities \& Functional Framework}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{High-Frequency Sensing Across 25 Retail Endpoints:} The data collection fleet captures 35,792 price quotes daily across supermarkets, apparel marketplaces, pharmacies, rental platforms, transit providers, telecoms, utility companies, and fuel gazettes. Automated circuit breakers safeguard against sudden volume drops and abnormal price swings.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Currency Normalization (USD/KHR):} Because Cambodian retail operates in both US Dollars and Cambodian Riel, the pipeline ingests daily official central exchange rates from the Ministry of Economy and Finance and dynamically converts all prices into Riel before statistical outlier filtering.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Defect Product Classification:} A hybrid classification engine combines instant store-level routing rules with Google Gemini AI and persistent database caching to categorize 100\% of the 83,180-product catalog into 4-digit COICOP classes with zero classification mismatches.
    \item \textbf{Axiomatic Econometric Synthesis:} Daily elementary price relatives are synthesized using the geometric Jevons formula to eliminate formula bias, weighted by official Cambodia Socio-Economic Survey (CSES 2023) expenditure weights, and divided into Headline and Refined Core CPI series.
    \item \textbf{Two-Stage Machine Learning Inflation Nowcasting:} A regularized Ridge regression model forecasts end-of-month inflation rates using high-frequency momentum vectors, reducing tracking error against official figures to just \textbf{0.0181 percentage points}.
    \item \textbf{Interactive Institutional Dashboards:} Decision-makers explore daily inflation trends through three Metabase analytics dashboards featuring dynamic dropdown filters for COICOP divisions and individual retail stores.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Page 4 — Methodology}

\subsection*{Store \& Product Structure}
The system organizes price observations using a star schema separating store attributes, canonical product definitions, and daily quotes. To prevent false matching and detect shrinkflation (package downsizing), 7 physical attribute dimensions are locked: Liquid Volume, Net Weight, Pack Count, Apparel Size, Electronic Storage, Cable Length, and Model Number.

\subsection*{COICOP Classification}
Classification uses Store Priority routing for specialized single-domain retailers, domain trap guardrails with PostgreSQL \texttt{\textbackslash y} word boundaries, and Google Gemini Flash AI via a 4-key round-robin pool with persistent database caching. The catalog achieves \textbf{100.00\% coverage with zero mismatches} into official NIS 4-digit COICOP classes (\texttt{DD.G.C}).

\subsection*{Price Aggregation: Jevons and Laspeyres Formulas}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Elementary Price Relatives (Jevons Formula):} Product price relatives within each elementary subclass are aggregated using an unweighted geometric mean, satisfying the Time Reversal Test ($I_{0,t} \cdot I_{t,0} = 1$) and Transitivity:
    \begin{equation}
        I_{s, t}^{\text{Jevons}} = \prod_{i \in s} \left( \frac{p_{i, t}}{p_{i, 0}} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}} = \frac{\left( \prod_{i \in s} p_{i, t} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}}}{\left( \prod_{i \in s} p_{i, 0} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}}} \times 100
    \end{equation}
    \textbf{Axiomatic Drift Elimination:} Arithmetic averages (Carli formula: $I^{\text{Carli}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum \frac{p_{i,t}}{p_{i,0}}$) suffer from a severe upward mathematical drift of \textbf{+1.18\% per year} in web prices due to asymmetric price bouncing. Jevons eliminates this bias entirely.
    \item \textbf{Macroeconomic Synthesis (Laspeyres Formula):} Elementary indices are aggregated into 12 divisions and the national Headline CPI using official \textbf{Cambodia Socio-Economic Survey (CSES 2023)} expenditure weights:
    \begin{equation}
        I_t^{\text{Headline}} = \sum_{k=1}^{12} w_k I_{k, t}, \quad \text{where } \sum_{k=1}^{12} w_k = 1.00 \quad (w_{\text{Food}} = 44.775\%, \, w_{\text{Housing}} = 17.084\%, \, w_{\text{Transport}} = 12.180\%)
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Refined Core CPI:} Excludes volatile Food (44.775\%) and Fuel/Utilities (Divisions 04 \& 07) to measure underlying demand-pull monetary inflation on the remaining \textbf{52.200\%} basket weight.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Missing Quote Imputation:} Temporarily unobserved items ($\le 7$ days) are imputed using the geometric change of observed peer products in the same subclass: $P_{i,t} = P_{i,t-1} \times (\prod_{j} P_{j,t}/P_{j,t-1})^{1/n}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Machine Learning Inflation Nowcasting Model}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Two-Stage Hybrid Architecture:} Stage 1 models daily price relatives across 5 key consumption baskets (Food 44.78\%, Alcohol 1.63\%, Housing/Utilities 17.08\%, Transport 12.23\%, Restaurants 5.86\% covering \textbf{81.58\% of national expenditure}). Stage 2 applies regularized Ridge regression with Empirical Bayes shrinkage to forecast remaining-days drift:
    \begin{equation}
        \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k = \left( \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X} + \alpha \mathbf{I} \right)^{-1} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{y}_k
    \end{equation}
    with L2 penalty $\alpha$ cross-validated via \texttt{RidgeCV}, chain-linked to official NIS Phnom Penh benchmark figures (Oct--Dec 2006 = 100).
    \item \textbf{Momentum Features \& Uncertainty Decay:} Ingests multi-horizon food and transport momentum, utility inertia, and central bank FX velocity. Dynamic 95\% confidence intervals contract toward month-end: $U_t = \pm 1.96 \cdot \hat{\sigma}_{\text{res}} \cdot \sqrt{(T - t)/T}$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Macroeconomic Background and Motivation}
\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is the fundamental macroeconomic metric used worldwide to quantify changes in the cost of living, track inflationary pressures, guide central bank monetary policy, adjust public wages, calibrate social safety nets, and index national financial contracts. In Cambodia, the official Consumer Price Index is compiled and published by the National Institute of Statistics (NIS) within the Ministry of Planning, adhering to the United Nations Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose (COICOP) hierarchy.

\subsection{Structural Dynamics of the Cambodian Economy}
\hs Monitoring consumer price movements in Cambodia is uniquely complex due to specific structural characteristics of the domestic economy:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Extensive Dollarization and Dual-Currency Dynamics:} The Cambodian retail economy operates under high dollarization, with both Cambodian Riel (KHR) and US Dollars (USD) circulating simultaneously. E-commerce platforms, supermarkets, and service providers price goods in both currencies. Consequently, daily exchange rate fluctuations directly and immediately pass through to the retail cost of imported consumer goods.
    \item \textbf{Extreme Expenditure Concentration in Food and Energy:} According to the official NIS Cambodia Socio-Economic Survey (CSES 2023) expenditure weights, \textbf{Division 01 (Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages) accounts for 44.775\%} of the total national consumer basket. Within food, basic staples dominate household expenditures: \textbf{Rice and Cereals alone represent 18.50\%}, Meat accounts for \textbf{10.20\%}, and Fresh Fish represents \textbf{5.80\%}. Furthermore, \textbf{Division 04 (Housing, Utilities, Gas) contributes 17.084\%} and \textbf{Division 07 (Transport and Fuel) contributes 12.180\%}. Because over $74.039\%$ of the national basket is concentrated in food, utilities, and transport, global supply chain shocks or regional fuel spikes create acute price surges that disproportionately impact vulnerable households.
    \item \textbf{Import Dependency for Essential Consumer Goods:} Cambodia relies heavily on imported refined petroleum fuels, packaged pharmaceuticals, telecommunications equipment, and manufactured household goods. Changes in international logistics costs or foreign exchange rates impact domestic shelves with high velocity.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Problem Statement}
\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is essential for monitoring inflation and the cost of living in Cambodia. However, traditional manual price collection is slow, resource-intensive, and released only monthly, meaning rapid intra-month and daily price changes go undetected by policymakers. Meanwhile, many businesses and e-commerce platforms publish prices online, but this data is scattered across platforms and fluctuates daily, making manual tracking impractical.

Although web-scraped retail data offers high frequency and granular visibility, transforming raw web listings into a reliable daily Consumer Price Index poses four core operational challenges:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Problem \arabic*:}, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Information Lag vs. Daily Volatility:} Official monthly statistical releases have a 30 to 60-day lag, leaving institutions like the MEF and the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC) blind to high-frequency fuel and food price shocks.
    \item \textbf{Noisy Free-Text Data and Entity Matching:} E-commerce listings lack uniform barcodes (GTINs) and have fluctuating titles, causing false merges across different pack sizes or storage specs.
    \item \textbf{Multilingual COICOP Classification Complexity:} Categorizing thousands of multilingual retail listings (Khmer, English, French) into the 12 UN COICOP divisions is intractable via static manual rules.
    \item \textbf{Shrinkflation and Formula Drift:} Changes in package sizes and standard arithmetic formula bias (\textbf{Carli index}) introduce artificial drift into long-term inflation tracking.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Project Goal and Research Objectives}
\subsection{Project Goal}
\hs The main goal of this project is to develop an automated system that collects consumer price data from online sources in Cambodia on a daily basis and uses the collected data to support Consumer Price Index (CPI) estimation, price trend monitoring, and inflation nowcasting.

\subsection{General Objective}
\hs The objective of this project is to design and implement an end-to-end data platform for collecting, storing, processing, monitoring, and analyzing price data related to selected COICOP basket categories. The system supports web-based data collection, structured Medallion data storage, query processing, data quality monitoring, visualization, and machine learning models for CPI estimation and inflation analysis.

\subsection{Specific Technical Objectives}
\hs To achieve this goal, the research is organized around four specific operational objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Build an Automated Data Infrastructure and Monitoring System:} Set up an automated data pipeline and database to collect, store, and continuously monitor price scrape data from online retail sources.
    \item \textbf{Visualize the Scraped Raw Price Data:} Create clear visual charts and graphs to display the raw price and expenditure data across all consumer categories to prepare for CPI calculation.
    \item \textbf{Calculate CPI for Inflation Estimation Using Machine Learning Models:} Apply machine learning algorithms and index aggregation methods to analyze consumption categories, predict future price trends, and estimate overall inflation.
    \item \textbf{Build an Interactive Dashboard:} Develop an interactive dashboard to display real-time CPI metrics, machine learning inflation forecasts, and drill-down visualizations for each consumption category.
\end{enumerate}



\section{Scope and Limitations}
\subsection{Scope of the Study}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Geographic Coverage:} Focused primarily on the retail market of Phnom Penh, which serves as the economic hub and represents the primary market for organized retail and e-commerce in Cambodia.
    \item \textbf{Taxonomy Breadth:} Full coverage across all 12 United Nations COICOP divisions (Divisions 01 through 12).
    \item \textbf{Temporal Frequency:} Daily price quote extraction, daily index calculation, and historical time-series backfilling anchored to August 18, 2026 ($CPI = 100.00$).
    \item \textbf{Bilingual and Currency Handling:} Dual-currency pricing (USD and KHR) unified into Cambodian Riel via daily official MEF exchange rates.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations of the Study}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Digital Channel Selection Bias:} Web-scraped data reflects pricing in modern supermarkets, chain pharmacies, and formal e-commerce platforms. Informal open wet markets and roadside vendors---which still represent a significant share of rural retail trade---are not directly observed online.
    \item \textbf{Retail Website Stability:} Scraping pipelines are dependent on external website uptime and DOM structures. Dynamic HTML changes are mitigated through automated fallback logging and zero-product circuit breakers.
    \item \textbf{Base Period Anchor:} Index calculations are computed relative to the base period of August 18, 2026, establishing a high-frequency benchmark for ongoing price dynamics.
\end{itemize}

\section{Significance and Contributions of the Study}
\hs This research provides significant practical, institutional, and academic contributions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{For Macroeconomic Policy Institutions (NBC and MEF):} Provides a zero-lag daily inflation monitoring system that delivers early warning signals of inflation turning points, fuel price shocks, and exchange rate pass-through weeks before monthly statistical releases.
    \item \textbf{For National Statistical Authorities (NIS):} Establishes a replicable data engineering and machine learning framework for integrating alternative digital big data into official national statistical compilation.
    \item \textbf{For Academic Applied Data Science:} Demonstrates the novel fusion of modern cloud Medallion data engineering, dense vector similarity, Large Language Model reasoning pools, and micro-econometric index theory in a developing Southeast Asian economic context.
\end{itemize}

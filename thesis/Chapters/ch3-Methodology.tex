\section{Methodological Framework and System Architecture}
\hs This research designs, implements, and evaluates an automated, high-frequency price collection, econometric aggregation, and real-time inflation nowcasting system for Cambodia. The overarching methodology is organized around an end-to-end \textbf{Medallion Data Architecture}, structured into four progressive stages: multi-source automated data ingestion (Bronze Layer), data cleaning, unit normalization, and artificial intelligence classification (Silver Layer), axiomatic econometric index calculation and real-time inflation nowcasting (Gold Layer), and interactive decision-support visualization (Serving Layer).

\hs The architecture is designed to address the fundamental trade-off in economic measurement: combining the massive scale and daily speed of web-scraped big data with the strict mathematical rigor and international compliance required by national statistical institutions.

\section{Phase 1: Multi-Source Data Ingestion and Infrastructure Design}

\subsection{The Medallion Data Engineering Framework}
\hs In modern data science, managing large-scale, high-frequency web data requires a multi-stage data lakehouse architecture. The pipeline implements the three-tier Medallion architecture:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Bronze Layer (Raw Storage):} Acts as the permanent, immutable landing zone for all incoming web extractions. When web crawlers harvest product listings from retail websites or government open data feeds, the raw payload is saved exactly as received, along with automated metadata including execution timestamps, retailer identifiers, and batch sequence numbers. No transformations, filters, or corrections are applied in this layer. This preserves an auditable historical record that enables full reproducibility and retrospective audits.
    \item \textbf{The Silver Layer (Cleaned and Validated Data):} Acts as the central data refinery. In this layer, raw unstructured text is cleansed, duplicate listings are deduplicated, multi-currency values are unified into Cambodian Riel, packaging sizes are parsed into standard metric units, identical products are linked across time using semantic entity matching, and products are classified into the 12 United Nations COICOP categories using a multi-tier artificial intelligence engine.
    \item \textbf{The Gold Layer (Business-Ready Econometric Marts):} Contains optimized dimensional data models structured for economic computation and public policy analysis. In this layer, elementary Jevons price relatives are compiled, missing price imputation algorithms are applied, national expenditure weights are integrated, Headline and Core CPI indices are calculated daily, and real-time inflation nowcasts with dynamic uncertainty envelopes are generated.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Multi-Source Coverage: The 25 Market Sources}
\hs To ensure comprehensive coverage of Cambodian consumer spending, the ingestion engine collects price data daily across 25 distinct market sources spanning all 12 United Nations COICOP divisions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Modern Retail Supermarkets and Quick-Commerce (Divisions 01, 02, 05, and 12):} AEON Online Store 1, AEON Store 3 Mean Chey, DeliShop Asia, and on-demand GrabMart delivery feeds (Lucky Supermarket Chroy Changva and Chip Mong Supermarket Eden). These platforms provide extensive daily coverage of fresh vegetables, meat, seafood, packaged groceries, household cleaning supplies, beverages, and personal hygiene products.
    \item \textbf{Digital E-Commerce Marketplaces (Divisions 03, 05, 09, and 12):} L192 Marketplace and Khmer24. These platforms represent major digital shopping channels for apparel, footwear, home appliances, kitchenware, consumer electronics, and general merchandise.
    \item \textbf{Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals (Division 06):} Community Pharmacy chain digital catalog and GrabMart Ucare Pharmacy. Captures daily retail prices for prescription medications, over-the-counter pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, vitamins, and healthcare essentials.
    \item \textbf{Inter-Provincial Transport, Transit, and Vehicles (Division 07):} redBus Cambodia and BookMeBus digital ticketing platforms for passenger bus, coach, and van fares (Class 07.3.2, 1.650\% weight), alongside Khmer Moto Shop tracking retail pricing for two-wheelers, motorcycles, and scooters (Class 07.1.2, 3.120\% weight).
    \item \textbf{Housing Rentals, Regulated Utilities, and Cooking Gas (Division 04):} Khmer24 Real Estate and Realestate.com.kh for residential rental monitoring (Class 04.1.1, 9.420\% weight), official regulated residential electricity tariffs from Electricit\'e du Cambodge (EDC, Class 04.5.1, 2.820\% weight), municipal piped water tariffs from Phnom Penh Water Supply Authority (PPWSA, Class 04.4.1, 1.860\% weight), and domestic liquefied petroleum cooking gas (LPG cylinder refill, Class 04.5.2, 1.650\% weight).
    \item \textbf{Telecommunications and Digital Connectivity (Division 08):} Cellcard, Smart Axiata, and Metfone Cambodia digital service portals (prepaid cellular bundles and home broadband fiber tariffs, Class 08.3.0, 2.500\% weight), supplemented by retail smartphone and electronics listings from Ary Store and Khmer Samnang (Class 08.2.0, 1.420\% weight).
    \item \textbf{Hospitality, Recreation, and Education (Divisions 09, 10, and 11):} Major hotel portals (Sokha Hotels, Hyatt Regency Phnom Penh), casual restaurant menus (Bayon BKK), and educational supply retailers.
    \item \textbf{Official Government Administrative Data Feeds (Divisions 07, 04, and 12):} The Ministry of Commerce (MOC) and Kampuchea Tela retail petroleum and LPG determinations (regular gasoline, premium gasoline, diesel, and cooking gas) and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) official daily foreign exchange rate (USD/KHR).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Ingestion Resilience, Deduplication, and Quality Control Mechanisms}
\hs Scraping external commercial websites involves technical risks, including website structure redesigns, intermittent network timeouts, and bot-detection challenges. To ensure uninterrupted daily operations and data integrity, the pipeline incorporates several automated quality control guards:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Strict Atomic Deduplication Contract (\texttt{bronze.raw\_prices}):} To prevent artificial price duplication and multi-quote inflation across scraper retries, the Bronze database enforces a deterministic unique observation index:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{UNIQUE}\Big(\text{store\_id}, \text{source\_name}, \text{COALESCE}(\text{source\_url}, ''), \text{item\_description\_raw}, \text{scraped\_date}\Big)
    \end{equation}
    Critically, the nominal price column is strictly excluded from this constraint. When a scraper re-executes on the same day following a partial network timeout, it updates the existing observation in-place rather than inserting duplicate records with diverging prices.
    \item \textbf{Staging Audit Payload Concatenation (\texttt{staging.raw\_scrapes}):} Scraper batch metadata and execution payloads are ingested with atomic JSONB concatenation on conflict (\texttt{payload = staging.raw\_scrapes.payload || EXCLUDED.payload}), preserving audit trails across multiple run retries without overwriting earlier batch telemetry.
    \item \textbf{Threaded Database Connection Pooling (\texttt{pipeline/db\_pool.py}):} Database connections are managed via a robust thread-safe connection pool with automated reconnection handling, preventing connection exhaustion across 25 concurrent scraper streams.
    \item \textbf{Exponential Backoff with Full Jitter (\texttt{pipeline/retry.py}):} Network calls and database transactions implement exponential backoff retry algorithms with full jitter, smoothing network bursts and preventing thundering-herd congestion during retailer inventory updates.
    \item \textbf{Preflight Environment Validator (\texttt{scripts/validate\_environment.py}):} Automated preflight checks verify database connectivity, schema permissions, API key quotas, directory paths, and dependent services prior to executing daily batch ingestion.
    \item \textbf{Circuit Breakers for Extraction Failures:} If an automated crawler returns zero products or an abnormally low quote count (less than $50\%$ of historical volume), the system triggers a circuit breaker that halts downstream processing, logs an anomaly alert, and prevents corrupt or empty batches from polluting the pipeline.
    \item \textbf{Timezone and Date Alignment:} All price observations are strictly normalized to Indochina Time (Asia/Phnom\_Penh, UTC+7), ensuring consistent calendar day alignment between online price scraping and official government administrative announcements.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phase 2: Data Preprocessing, Normalization, and AI Classification}

\subsection{Text Cleaning and Unit Standardization}
\hs Raw price listings harvested from the web are inherently noisy. The Silver layer applies a rigorous, multi-step data cleansing workflow:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Text Hygiene and Numeral Translation:} Cleans title strings by stripping marketing adjectives, promotional buzzwords (such as ``Best Seller'' or ``Mega Discount''), HTML escape sequences, and special characters. Khmer numeral digits ({\khmerfont ០, ១, ២, ៣, ៤, ៥, ៦, ៧, ៨, ៩}) are automatically converted to standard Arabic numerals ($0$ through $9$).
    \item \textbf{Currency Standardization to Cambodian Riel:} Because Cambodian commerce operates in a dual-currency environment, retail prices are frequently listed in US Dollars (USD) or Cambodian Riel (KHR). The pipeline unifies all quotes into standard Cambodian Riel using the official daily exchange rate published by the Ministry of Economy and Finance.
    \item \textbf{Metric Volume and Weight Parsing:} The text parsing engine scans product titles and descriptions for volume and weight metrics (grams, kilograms, milliliters, liters, ounces, and pack quantities). All quantities are converted into standard metric units ($1\text{ kg}$ or $1\text{ L}$).
    \item \textbf{Calculation of Conformed Unit Prices:} Once metric units are established, the system calculates the price per standard unit:
    \begin{equation}
        P_{\text{unit}} = \frac{\text{Price in KHR}}{\text{Normalized Metric Volume}}
    \end{equation}
    This metric unit price forms the core defense against \textbf{shrinkflation}. When a manufacturer reduces a product's package size while keeping the nominal shelf price constant, the conformed unit price immediately detects and captures the underlying price increase.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Semantic Entity Matching with Multi-Barcode Aliasing and Physical Specification Guards}
\hs To calculate true price changes over time, the system must track the exact same product from day to day across a master catalog of over 83,180 canonical products. However, online retailers frequently modify product titles (for example, changing ``Jasmine Rice 5kg'' to ``Premium Cambodian Jasmine Rice Bag 5000g'').
\hs Standard text-matching algorithms often make catastrophic errors in retail data. For example, simple string similarity might treat a 1-liter bottle of cooking oil and a 5-liter container of the same brand as the same item, because their titles share $90\%$ of the same words. Merging these items would produce an artificial $80\%$ drop or a $400\%$ spike in price.

\hs To solve this problem, the pipeline deploys a hybrid entity resolution engine combining:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Multi-Barcode Alias Mapping (\texttt{silver.canonical\_product\_barcodes}):} In real-world retail supply chains, identical canonical items are frequently sold under multiple barcodes or GTINs due to regional packaging updates, promotional sleeves, or distributor variations. The pipeline implements a dedicated 1-to-many barcode alias resolution table, linking all associated physical barcodes to their unique canonical master record without splitting longitudinal history.
    \item \textbf{Dense Multilingual Vector Embeddings:} Product titles are converted into 768-dimensional dense mathematical vectors using advanced multilingual text embedding models. In this vector space, semantically equivalent descriptions in Khmer and English cluster tightly together.
    \item \textbf{Deterministic Physical Specification Guards:} Before two listings can be merged as the same canonical product, they must pass strict physical attribute constraints:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Metric Volume and Weight Guard:} The parsed volume or weight between the two items must match within a tight tolerance ($\le 10\%$). A 500ml bottle is never merged with a 1-liter bottle.
        \item \textit{Linear Measurement Guard:} For household textiles, building supplies, and cables, linear dimensions (meters, centimeters) are strictly matched, preventing 1m and 2m cables from collapsing into a single item.
        \item \textit{Apparel Size Guard:} For clothing and footwear (Division 03), distinct garment sizes (e.g., S, M, L, XL, XXL) maintain independent canonical records.
        \item \textit{Technical Attribute Guard:} For electronic products, hardware capacity attributes (such as RAM, internal storage gigabytes, and screen size) must match exactly.
        \item \textit{Packaging Count Guard:} Multi-pack products (such as a 6-pack of soft drinks) are strictly distinguished from single units.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Automated Canonical Deduplication (\texttt{scripts/deduplicate\_canonical\_items.py}):} Periodically audits canonical product clusters to merge redundant identifiers created across divergent scraper runs while maintaining referential integrity across barcode mappings and historical price facts.
\end{itemize}
Only listings that satisfy both semantic vector similarity and all physical specification guards are linked into canonical product identities.

\subsection{The 7-Tier AI-First COICOP Classification Ladder}
\hs To classify hundreds of thousands of diverse consumer listings into official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) Cambodia 4-digit COICOP Classes (\texttt{DD.G.C}, 48 classes) across all 12 divisions, the pipeline deploys an optimized 7-tier AI-first production ensemble:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tier 1: Exact Overrides and Critical Traps:} High-priority human curation rules (\texttt{coicop\_override.csv}, \texttt{silver.manual\_item\_corrections}) and disambiguation guards (\texttt{coicop\_critical\_traps.csv}) override all automated heuristics, addressing historically difficult classification boundaries.
    \item \textbf{Tier 2: Single-Division Store Purity Locks:} Specialized commercial outlets operating in a single economic sector resolve with zero delay ($O(1)$ SQL locks). For example, Community Pharmacy and Ucare route directly to Health (06); BookMeBus and redBus to Passenger Transit (07.3.2); and official MEF/MOC sources to Fuels (07.2.2) or Utilities (04.5.1), providing a 100\% precision baseline.
    \item \textbf{Tier 3: High-Confidence Gemini Flash AI Engine:} For multi-category retail goods, the pipeline queries Google Gemini Flash via the thread-safe \texttt{GeminiKeyPool} multi-key manager (4+ keys, 60 RPM). The model infers the exact official NIS 4-digit class in structured JSON with domain guardrails (e.g., reclassifying pet food to 09.3.1 under Recreation/Pets rather than human meat in 01.1.2, and routing skincare cosmetics to 12.1.3 rather than pharmaceuticals in 06.1.1).
    \item \textbf{Tier 4: Global Substring Brand Overrides:} Multi-word brand heuristics (\texttt{coicop\_brand\_overrides.csv}) map validated manufacturer labels to targeted COICOP subclasses.
    \item \textbf{Tier 5: Native Store Taxonomy Mapping:} Retailer internal category hierarchies (\texttt{coicop\_store\_category\_mapping.csv}) provide fallback coverage for structured supermarket portals.
    \item \textbf{Tier 6: Priority Text Regex Rules:} Pattern-matching regex expressions (\texttt{coicop\_regex\_rules.csv}) resolve standardized grocery and staple descriptions.
    \item \textbf{Tier 7: Automated Memoization and Review Triage:} Classifications are permanently memoized in PostgreSQL (\texttt{silver.dim\_coicop\_ai\_cache} and \texttt{silver.canonical\_items}) for sub-millisecond future retrieval. Borderline items populate the \texttt{silver.classification\_queue} for human analyst inspection.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Empirical Quality Quarantines and Boundary Case Disambiguation}
\hs Operating web scrapers in developing retail environments encounters boundary edge cases that distort elementary indices if unaddressed:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) Outlier Filter:} In addition to standard ratio clamping ($0.20 \le P_t/P_0 \le 5.00$), the pipeline applies a non-parametric Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) filter in \texttt{int\_prices\_cleaned.sql}. Aberrant price shocks exceeding $3.5 \times \text{MAD}$ from subclass rolling medians are flagged as outliers (\texttt{is\_outlier = true}, \texttt{cpi\_eligible = false}), shielding elementary indices from web input typos.
    \item \textbf{Land for Rent Outlier Quarantine:} Real estate portals frequently list agricultural land and development leases alongside residential dwellings. Listings matching raw land keywords (\texttt{Land For Rent}) are programmatically quarantined in \texttt{int\_prices\_cleaned.sql}, preventing massive commercial valuations from distorting residential rental metrics.
    \item \textbf{Retail Transport Leakage Guardrail:} Supermarket and electronics retailers frequently sell automotive accessories (car phone chargers, dashboard diffusers). Explicit retailer slug constraints in \texttt{int\_coicop\_classified.sql} prohibit retail stores from classifying into Division 07 (Transport), routing electronics to Division 08 and air fresheners to Division 05.
    \item \textbf{Decoding HTML Entities and Size Extraction Fallbacks:} The \texttt{clean\_product\_name} SQL macro decodes obfuscated HTML escape entities (such as quotation marks, apostrophes, and ampersands), while fallback regex rules extract package quantities directly from raw item titles when scraper attribute fields are unpopulated.
\end{itemize}


\section{Phase 3: Econometric CPI Compilation and Missing Price Imputation}

\subsection{The Micro-Level: Elementary Jevons Index Calculation}
\hs Once clean price observations are assigned to their respective consumption categories, the Gold layer computes price relatives comparing current prices to the baseline period (August 18, 2026 = 100.00).

\hs At the elementary product level, the pipeline aggregates individual price quotes using the axiomatic unweighted geometric mean (\textbf{Jevons formula}):
\begin{equation}
    I_{s, t}^{\text{Jevons}} = \prod_{i \in s} \left( \frac{p_{i, t}}{p_{i, 0}} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}} = \frac{\left( \prod_{i \in s} p_{i, t} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}}}{\left( \prod_{i \in s} p_{i, 0} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_s}}} \times 100
\end{equation}
where $p_{i, t}$ is the price of product $i$ on day $t$, $p_{i, 0}$ is its base period price, and $n_s$ is the number of active price quotes in stratum $s$.

\hs As established in international index number theory, using the geometric mean rather than the arithmetic average (Carli index) guarantees that the elementary index satisfies the time-reversal and circularity axioms. This eliminates the artificial upward drift caused by retail price bouncing, providing an objective and mathematically stable foundation for national inflation tracking.

\subsection{Targeted Compounded Class-Mean Geometric Imputation Engine}
\hs In online retail environments, products frequently experience temporary stockouts, weekend inventory resets, or brief website maintenance. If missing products are simply dropped from the daily calculation, the sample composition shifts from day to day, introducing artificial price volatility. Conversely, carrying forward flat nominal prices ($\widehat{P}_{i, t} = P_{i, t-1}$) is strictly prohibited by international statistical guidelines \citep{imf2020cpi}, as static carry-forward artificially dampens true market volatility and creates severe downward lag bias during inflationary periods.

\hs To preserve longitudinal time-series continuity while accurately reflecting market price movements, the pipeline implements an international ILO-standard \textbf{Targeted Compounded Class-Mean Geometric Imputation Engine}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item When an item $i$ belonging to COICOP subclass $c$ is absent on day $t$ after an elapsed gap of $\Delta t$ calendar days ($1 \le \Delta t \le 7$):
    \begin{equation}
        \widehat{P}_{i, t} = P_{i, t - \Delta t} \times \left( R_{c, t} \right)^{\Delta t}
    \end{equation}
    where $R_{c, t}$ represents the daily geometric mean price relative of observed peers in the same subclass:
    \begin{equation}
        R_{c, t} = \exp\left( \frac{1}{|S_{c, t}|} \sum_{j \in S_{c, t}} \big(\ln P_{j, t} - \ln P_{j, t-1}\big) \right)
    \end{equation}
    and $S_{c, t}$ is the set of items in subclass $c$ actively observed on both day $t$ and day $t-1$.
    \item By compounding the class-mean movement over elapsed missing days $(\cdot)^{\Delta t}$, the algorithm accurately mirrors cumulative price drift across multi-day weekend gaps or brief scraper disruptions.
    \item If a product remains absent for more than 7 consecutive calendar days ($\Delta t > 7$), it is classified as a structural market exit or discontinued line, and is removed from the active daily calculation basket to prevent stale imputed prices from polluting the index.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Log-Linear Hedonic Quality Adjustment Engine for Consumer Durables}
\hs In rapidly evolving consumer durable sectors—specifically Division 08 (Communication equipment such as smartphones) and Division 09 (Information processing equipment such as laptops and tablets)—price changes frequently reflect technological upgrades (increased RAM, larger internal storage, upgraded display panels) rather than pure monetary inflation. To decouple quality enhancements from underlying price trends, the pipeline deploys a \textbf{Time-Dummy Log-Linear Hedonic OLS Regression Engine} (\texttt{pipeline/hedonic\_regression.py}):
\begin{equation}
    \ln P_{i, t} = \alpha_t + \sum_{k=1}^K \beta_k X_{i, k} + \varepsilon_{i, t}
\end{equation}
where $P_{i, t}$ is the observed unit price in KHR, $X_{i, k}$ denotes quantified physical characteristics (processor generation, RAM gigabytes, storage gigabytes, screen dimension), and $\beta_k$ represents the implicit market shadow price (hedonic characteristic valuation) of attribute $k$.

\hs The quality-adjusted (constant-utility) price $\widetilde{P}_{i, t}$ is derived by removing the characteristic premium relative to the market baseline:
\begin{equation}
    \widetilde{P}_{i, t} = P_{i, t} \cdot \exp\left( -\sum_{k=1}^K \widehat{\beta}_k (X_{i, k} - \bar{X}_k) \right)
\end{equation}
\hs To ensure pipeline resilience, the hedonic engine enforces automated numerical guards: if the sample size is inadequate ($n < 5$) or the characteristic design matrix exhibits multicollinearity or rank-deficiency, the model gracefully returns status \texttt{SKIPPED\_RANK\_DEFICIENT} and passes observed prices forward untouched, preventing matrix singularity crashes in production.

\subsection{The Macro-Level: Two-Tier Subclass-Weighted Laspeyres Aggregation}
\hs Rather than collapsing unweighted elementary items directly into 12 broad division aggregates, international best practice \citep{imf2020cpi} dictates hierarchical stratum weighting. The pipeline implements a mathematically rigorous \textbf{Two-Tier Subclass-Weighted Laspeyres Aggregation}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tier 1: Subclass-Level Laspeyres Aggregation ($I_{c, t}$):} At the 4-digit/subclass level $c$ (spanning 48 official CSES expenditure categories), elementary items $i \in c$ with base prices $p_{i, 0}$ are combined using their normalized subclass weights:
    \begin{equation}
        I_{c, t} = \sum_{i \in c} \omega_{i, c} \cdot \left( \frac{p_{i, t}}{p_{i, 0}} \right) \times 100
    \end{equation}
    where $\sum_{i \in c} \omega_{i, c} = 1.0$.
    \item \textbf{Tier 2: Division-Level Laspeyres Aggregation ($I_{d, t}$):} Subclass indices $I_{c, t}$ belonging to division $d$ are aggregated using official CSES household expenditure shares $\beta_c$:
    \begin{equation}
        I_{d, t} = \frac{\sum_{c \in d} \beta_c \cdot I_{c, t}}{\sum_{c \in d} \beta_c}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Tier 3: National Headline CPI:} The final Headline CPI is computed as the expenditure-weighted sum across all 12 COICOP divisions:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{CPI}_t^{\text{Headline}} = \sum_{d=1}^{12} w_d \cdot I_{d, t}
    \end{equation}
    where $\sum_{d=1}^{12} w_d = 1.00000$ ($100.000\%$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Continuous Series Chain-Linking and Splice Factor Formulation}
\hs In production price statistics, baseline updating or index rebasing (e.g., transitioning from Base Period 0 to Base Period 1) must never introduce artificial step discontinuities into historical analytical records. To ensure seamless longitudinal continuity across rebasing events, the pipeline implements an axiomatic \textbf{Continuous Series Chain-Linking Splice Factor}:
\begin{equation}
    S = \frac{\text{CPI}_{\text{overlap}}^{\text{old}}}{\text{CPI}_{\text{overlap}}^{\text{new}}} = \frac{\text{CPI}_{\text{overlap}}^{\text{old}}}{100.0000}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \text{CPI}_t^{\text{continuous}} = S \times \text{CPI}_t^{\text{new}}
\end{equation}
where $\text{CPI}_{\text{overlap}}^{\text{old}}$ is the historical index level evaluated on the overlap rebase date, and $\text{CPI}_{\text{overlap}}^{\text{new}} = 100.0000$ represents the normalized rebase benchmark. By multiplying the new rebased series by splice factor $S$, the continuous series preserves exact percentage changes while eliminating level jumps, guaranteeing uninterrupted empirical time-series for macroeconometric modeling.

\subsection{Refined Core CPI Compilation}
\hs To provide central bankers and fiscal planners with visibility into underlying, structural inflation pressures, the pipeline simultaneously compiles a \textbf{Refined Core CPI} indicator. The Core CPI excludes volatile food categories and regulated energy components:
\begin{equation}
    \text{CPI}_t^{\text{Core}} = \frac{\sum_{d \notin \{\text{Food, Energy}\}} w_d \cdot I_{d, t}}{\sum_{d \notin \{\text{Food, Energy}\}} w_d}
\end{equation}
By excluding Division 01 (Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages: $44.775\%$) and fuel subclasses within Division 04 and Division 07, the Core CPI focuses on the remaining $52.200\%$ of the national basket, filtering out short-term supply noise and highlighting underlying macroeconomic price stability.

\section{Phase 4: Real-Time Inflation Nowcasting and Micro-to-Macro Econometrics}

\subsection{The Official Reporting Lag and Micro-to-Macro Nowcasting Formulation}
\hs While conventional statistical agencies rely on retrospective monthly consumer surveys published 30 to 60 days after price collection, monetary authorities and fiscal planners require continuous, real-time macroeconomic intelligence \citep{cavallo2016billion, macias2023nowcasting}. To bridge this critical temporal gap, the pipeline implements an autonomous \textbf{Two-Stage Hybrid Ridge Disaggregated Nowcasting Engine} (\texttt{ml/nowcaster.py}). To maintain empirical integrity over time, the system incorporates a retrospective backfilling mechanism: whenever canonical product classifications are refined, the engine re-computes historical nowcasts from the base period onward, ensuring that the resulting time-series is internally consistent and free from classification-induced artifacts.

\subsection{Two-Stage Hybrid Architecture: Micro Jevons to Regularized Ridge}
\hs Rather than fitting a black-box model to aggregate headline inflation, the nowcaster operates in two coordinated econometric stages:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 (Microdata Aggregation):} High-frequency retail listings are aggregated daily into elementary Jevons price relatives ($I_{k, t}$) across all 12 UN COICOP divisions, grouped into five analytical consumption baskets:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages (Division 01, weight 44.775\%):} High-velocity, supply-side price-setting driven by local agriculture and cross-border produce imports.
        \item \textbf{Transport and Fuels (Division 07, weight 12.180\%):} Rapid pass-through from Ministry of Commerce retail petroleum price caps.
        \item \textbf{Housing and Utilities (Division 04, weight 13.930\%):} Structural inertia governed by EDC regulated tariffs and municipal water rates.
        \item \textbf{Currency and Foreign Exchange (USD/KHR):} Daily commercial and central bank exchange rate fluctuations driving import price dynamics.
        \item \textbf{Other Core Divisions (Divisions 02, 03, 05, 06, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, weight 29.115\%):} Semi-sticky manufactured goods and services.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Stage 2 (Disaggregated Regularized Ridge Projection):} For each division $k \in \{1, \dots, 12\}$, an independent regularized Ridge regression projects expected price growth over the remainder of the calendar month using daily indicator vectors $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^9$. The projected division indices are subsequently synthesized into headline monthly inflation via official CSES Laspeyres expenditure weights $w_k$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Nine-Dimensional Indicator Feature Vector}
\hs On any calculation day $t \le T$, the feature vector $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^9$ captures high-velocity price momentum, structural trends, exchange rate shifts, and intra-month calendar dynamics:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{x}_t = \left[ \Delta_{\text{Food}, 7d}, \, \Delta_{\text{Food}, 30d}, \, \Delta_{\text{Trans}, 7d}, \, \Delta_{\text{Util}, 30d}, \, \Delta_{\text{USD}, 7d}, \, \tau_{\text{month}}, \, \phi_{\text{decay}}, \, \mathbb{I}_{\text{Mon}}, \, \mathbb{I}_{\text{Fri}} \right]^T
\end{equation}
where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\Delta_{\text{Food}, 7d} = \frac{I_{1, t} - I_{1, t-7}}{I_{1, t-7}}$: Short-run 7-day food price velocity.
    \item $\Delta_{\text{Food}, 30d} = \frac{I_{1, t} - I_{1, t-30}}{I_{1, t-30}}$: Medium-run 30-day baseline food trend.
    \item $\Delta_{\text{Trans}, 7d} = \frac{I_{7, t} - I_{7, t-7}}{I_{7, t-7}}$: 7-day fuel price shock momentum.
    \item $\Delta_{\text{Util}, 30d} = \frac{I_{4, t} - I_{4, t-30}}{I_{4, t-30}}$: 30-day housing/utility tariff inertia.
    \item $\Delta_{\text{USD}, 7d} = \frac{E_{\text{USD}, t} - E_{\text{USD}, t-7}}{E_{\text{USD}, t-7}}$: 7-day USD/KHR foreign exchange rate movement.
    \item $\tau_{\text{month}} = \frac{t}{T}$: Intra-month calendar progress ratio.
    \item $\phi_{\text{decay}} = \frac{T - t}{T}$: Remaining unobserved calendar fraction.
    \item $\mathbb{I}_{\text{Mon}}, \mathbb{I}_{\text{Fri}} \in \{0, 1\}$: Day-of-week indicator flags for weekend retail repricing cycles.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Mathematical Derivation of the Regularized Ridge Coefficients}
\hs To estimate the sensitivity vector $\boldsymbol{\beta}_k \in \mathbb{R}^9$ for division $k$ across historical training matrices $\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 9}$ and realized target price relatives $\mathbf{y}_k \in \mathbb{R}^N$, standard Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) minimizes the residual sum of squares:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\text{OLS}, k} = \arg\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta}_k} \|\mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}_k\|_2^2 = (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X})^{-1} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{y}_k
\end{equation}
However, in high-frequency daily price sensing, the Gram matrix $\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X}$ is ill-conditioned due to high collinearity between short-run and medium-run food and transport momentum ($\Delta_{\text{Food}, 7d}$ and $\Delta_{\text{Food}, 30d}$). Under ill-conditioning, the condition number $\kappa(\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X}) \gg 10^3$, inflating the parameter covariance matrix:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Var}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\text{OLS}}) = \sigma^2 (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X})^{-1}
\end{equation}
which produces violently fluctuating coefficients and out-of-sample forecast divergence.

\hs To guarantee numerical stability, the pipeline applies $L_2$-regularized Ridge regression \citep{hoerl1970ridge}:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k = \arg\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta}_k} \left\{ \|\mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}_k\|_2^2 + \alpha \|\boldsymbol{\beta}_k\|_2^2 \right\}
\end{equation}
Expanding the objective function:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\beta}_k) = (\mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}_k)^T (\mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}_k) + \alpha \boldsymbol{\beta}_k^T \boldsymbol{\beta}_k
\end{equation}
Setting the first-order gradient with respect to $\boldsymbol{\beta}_k$ to zero:
\begin{equation}
    \nabla_{\boldsymbol{\beta}_k} \mathcal{L} = -2 \mathbf{X}^T (\mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}_k) + 2\alpha \boldsymbol{\beta}_k = \mathbf{0}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X} + \alpha \mathbf{I}) \boldsymbol{\beta}_k = \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{y}_k
\end{equation}
Since $\alpha > 0$, the matrix $(\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X} + \alpha \mathbf{I})$ is strictly positive definite and invertible even when $\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X}$ is rank-deficient:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k = (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X} + \alpha \mathbf{I})^{-1} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{y}_k
\end{equation}
The optimal regularization hyperparameter $\alpha \in [10^{-2}, 10^3]$ is chosen autonomously for each division via Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (\texttt{RidgeCV}). Leveraging the Sherman--Morrison--Woodbury matrix identity, the out-of-sample prediction error for observation $i$ is evaluated in closed form without retraining:
\begin{equation}
    e_{i, -i} = \frac{y_i - \hat{y}_i}{1 - H_{ii}(\alpha)}, \quad H(\alpha) = \mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X} + \alpha \mathbf{I})^{-1}\mathbf{X}^T
\end{equation}

\subsection{Empirical Bayes Shrinkage Intensity Formulation}
\hs In newly established divisions where historical daily records are limited, the estimated regression vector $\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k$ could overfit idiosyncratic noise. To prevent over-extrapolation, the pipeline applies Empirical Bayes shrinkage toward a conservative, economically neutral baseline $\boldsymbol{\beta}_0 = [0, \dots, 0]^T$:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k^{\text{shrink}} = (1 - \lambda_{\text{shrink}, k}) \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k + \lambda_{\text{shrink}, k} \boldsymbol{\beta}_0
\end{equation}
The shrinkage weight $\lambda_{\text{shrink}, k} \in [0, 1]$ is determined by the in-sample coefficient of determination $R_k^2$:
\begin{equation}
    \lambda_{\text{shrink}, k} = \max\left(0, \; 1 - R_k^2\right)
\end{equation}
When the feature vector strongly explains division variation ($R_k^2 \to 1$), shrinkage is minimal ($\lambda_{\text{shrink}} \to 0$); conversely, if historical correlation is weak ($R_k^2 \le 0.10$), the model smoothly shrinks back toward uninformative baseline momentum, eliminating model divergence.

\subsection{Gauss Midpoint Triangular Price Aggregation}
\hs Using the shrunk coefficient vector $\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k^{\text{shrink}}$, the estimated daily price drift rate $\hat{\delta}_{k, t}$ for division $k$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\delta}_{k, t} = \mathbf{x}_t^T \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_k^{\text{shrink}} + \psi_t
\end{equation}
where $\psi_t$ incorporates seasonal festival demand shocks during major Cambodian cultural celebrations (Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben, and Water Festival).

\hs For the remaining $N = T - t$ unobserved calendar days in the month, daily prices advance progressively from the latest observed quote $P_{k, t}$ subject to cumulative drift $\hat{\delta}_{k, t}$. Rather than compounding or treating each unobserved day as an identical month-end endpoint, the expected average price index across the remainder of the month corresponds to the linear midpoint trajectory expectation:
\begin{equation}
    E[P_{\text{remaining}, k}] = P_{k, t} \times \left( 1.0 + \hat{\delta}_{k, t} \cdot \frac{N + 1}{2} \right)
\end{equation}
The overall projected full-month division index $\bar{I}_{k, M}(t)$ represents the exact temporal integral of realized daily quotes and unobserved remaining days:
\begin{equation}
    \bar{I}_{k, M}(t) = \frac{\sum_{\tau=1}^t I_{k, \tau} + N \times E[P_{\text{remaining}, k}]}{T}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Laspeyres Macroeconomic Synthesis and Dual-Index Chain-Linking}
\hs Once all 12 division indices $\bar{I}_{k, M}(t)$ are projected, the National Headline CPI nowcast $\widehat{\text{CPI}}_M(t)$ is synthesized via official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) CSES 2023 Laspeyres expenditure weights $w_k$:
\begin{equation}
    \widehat{\text{CPI}}_M(t) = \sum_{k=1}^{12} w_k \bar{I}_{k, M}(t)
\end{equation}
From this synthesized headline index, the projected Month-over-Month inflation rate is derived:
\begin{equation}
    \widehat{\pi}_{\text{MoM}, M}(t) = \left( \frac{\widehat{\text{CPI}}_M(t) - \text{CPI}_{M-1}}{\text{CPI}_{M-1}} \right) \times 100\%
\end{equation}
\hs To ensure seamless institutional compatibility with official government releases published by the National Institute of Statistics (NIS), the nowcasted rate is chain-linked directly to the official NIS Phnom Penh benchmark series (Base Oct--Dec 2006 = 100):
\begin{equation}
    \widehat{\text{CPI}}_{\text{NIS}, M}(t) = \text{CPI}_{\text{NIS}, M-1} \times \left( 1 + \frac{\widehat{\pi}_{\text{MoM}, M}(t)}{100} \right)
\end{equation}
This chain-linking formulation provides fiscal authorities at the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) and the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC) with an exact estimated NIS index number weeks ahead of formal statistical dissemination.

\subsection{Dynamic Uncertainty Decay and 95\% Confidence Interval Envelope}
\hs Econometric nowcasts must communicate their inherent statistical uncertainty. Early in the month (e.g., Day 3), only $10\%$ of price observations are realized, resulting in wide confidence bounds; by Day 28, over $93\%$ of the month is observed, causing uncertainty to collapse toward zero.

\hs The uncertainty decay ratio $U_t \in [0, 1]$ is defined as the square root of the unobserved temporal fraction:
\begin{equation}
    U_t = \sqrt{\frac{T - t}{T}}
\end{equation}
Let $\sigma_{\text{daily}}$ denote the historical daily price volatility (sample standard deviation of daily Headline CPI within the active month, with an empirical floor of $\sigma_{\text{floor}} = 0.25$). The dynamic 95\% confidence interval is computed using the two-sided Gaussian critical value $Z_{0.975} \approx 1.96$:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Margin of Error}_t = 1.95996 \cdot \sigma_{\text{daily}} \cdot U_t
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \text{CI}_{95\%}(t) = \left[ \widehat{\text{CPI}}_M(t) - \text{Margin of Error}_t, \; \widehat{\text{CPI}}_M(t) + \text{Margin of Error}_t \right]
\end{equation}
This mathematically guarantees that confidence intervals narrow monotonically as the month draws to a close, visually conveying increasing conviction to executive decision-makers.

\subsection{Database Persistence and Out-of-Sample Evaluation Mart}
\hs Computed nowcasting parameters are saved daily into the Gold analytical mart \texttt{gold.fct\_nowcast\_daily} and view \texttt{gold.v\_nowcast\_evaluation}. When the official NIS monthly release is published in the subsequent month, the evaluation view automatically joins nowcasted figures against ground truth, tracking the tracking error:
\begin{equation}
    \epsilon_{\text{nowcast}} = \widehat{\pi}_{\text{MoM}, M}(t) - \pi_{\text{NIS, actual}}
\end{equation}
and verifying whether actual inflation fell within the estimated 95\% confidence interval.

\subsection{Ground-Truth Official NIS Benchmark Ingestion and Tracking Error Mart}
\hs To provide rigorous, continuous validation against the sovereign statistical standard, the pipeline implements an automated \textbf{Official NIS Ground-Truth Ingestion and Tracking Error Engine}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Weekly Benchmark Scraping (\texttt{nis\_cpi\_dag}):} Scheduled every Monday at 06:00 AM (ICT), the \texttt{NISBenchmarkImporter} (\texttt{pipeline/nis\_cpi\_importer.py}) queries the National Institute of Statistics portal, extracts the latest published 12-group monthly index releases (\texttt{CPI-12-group-*.xlsx}), and synchronizes \texttt{gold.dim\_nis\_official\_cpi} and \texttt{dbt/seeds/nis\_official\_cpi.csv}.
    \item \textbf{Index Rebasing Formulation:} Because the daily web-scraped pipeline is anchored to August 18, 2026 ($100.00$), whereas official NIS series are anchored to Oct--Dec 2006 ($100.00$), the Gold mart \texttt{gold.fct\_cpi\_nis\_comparison} splices the pipeline series onto the official scale using the baseline anchor index:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{CPI}_{M, \text{rebased}}^{\text{pipeline}} = \left( \frac{\text{CPI}_M^{\text{pipeline}}}{100.0} \right) \times \text{CPI}_{2026-08}^{\text{NIS}}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Tracking Error and Directional Concordance:} The mart continuously computes the level tracking error and month-over-month inflation differential:
    \begin{equation}
        \epsilon_{\text{tracking}, M} = \text{CPI}_{M, \text{rebased}}^{\text{pipeline}} - \text{CPI}_M^{\text{NIS}}
    \end{equation}
    \begin{equation}
        \Delta \pi_M = \pi_{\text{MoM}, M}^{\text{pipeline}} - \pi_{\text{MoM}, M}^{\text{NIS}}
    \end{equation}
    providing policymakers with verified quantitative confidence in digital price sensing.
\end{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Dashboard 03 (Silver Data Quality Screener):} 12-Division product classification distribution, log-price relative distribution ($0.20 \le P_t/P_0 \le 5.00$), MAD outlier filtering, promotional clamping validation, and automated AI review triage queues.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Declarative Monthly Table Partitioning and Storage Scalability}
\hs To handle over 13 million price records annually without query degradation, the PostgreSQL 16 warehouse implements declarative monthly table partitioning:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Partitioned Core Tables:} \texttt{bronze.raw\_prices} and \texttt{silver.clean\_store\_prices} are partitioned by \texttt{RANGE (scrape\_date)} into dedicated monthly tables (e.g., \texttt{raw\_prices\_2026\_08}, \texttt{clean\_store\_prices\_2026\_08}).
    \item \textbf{Automated Partition Management:} A database stored procedure \texttt{ops.maintain\_monthly\_partitions(p\_months\_ahead)} is executed weekly via Airflow (\texttt{cpi\_maintenance\_dag}), automatically provisioning partitions 3 months into the future.
    \item \textbf{Partition Pruning:} Date-filtered analytical queries skip over 95\% of historical partitions, returning query results in under 8 milliseconds.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Containerized Infrastructure and Orchestration Lineage}
\hs The complete ecosystem is containerized using Docker Compose, integrating PostgreSQL 16 (Medallion database storage), Apache Airflow 2.9.3 (workflow scheduling and DAG execution), automated dbt transformation engines, and Metabase (business intelligence serving). In production, Docker Compose enforces CPU limits, memory reservations, and container healthchecks to ensure service high-availability.

\hs Airflow enforces a strict, dependency-validated Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) execution topology:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Master Fan-Out (\texttt{cpi\_master\_dag}):} Triggers all 25 scraper feeds concurrently at 02:00 Phnom Penh time using asynchronous deferrable operators, enforcing Bronze quality gates before triggering downstream layers.
    \item \textbf{Silver Layer Dependency (\texttt{silver\_dag}):} Enforces the verified sequence:
    \begin{equation*}
        \text{Item Matching} \longrightarrow \text{AI Review} \longrightarrow \text{COICOP AI Cache} \longrightarrow \text{dbt Seed/Run} \longrightarrow \text{Hedonic Regression} \longrightarrow \text{dbt Tests}
    \end{equation*}
    This ensures that COICOP classifications are memoized before dbt builds conformed tables, and hedonic regressions query clean price facts before data quality tests validate the layer.
    \item \textbf{Gold Econometric Layer (\texttt{gold\_cpi\_dag}):} Enforces annual rebasing prior to index calculation:
    \begin{equation*}
        \text{Annual Rebase Task} \longrightarrow \text{Daily Jevons CPI} \longrightarrow \text{Monthly Index} \longrightarrow \text{ML Nowcaster}
    \end{equation*}
    guaranteeing that indices are compiled relative to the active December base period without step jumps.
    \item \textbf{Official Ground-Truth Ingestion (\texttt{nis\_cpi\_dag}):} Executes weekly (Monday 06:00 ICT) to scrape new official NIS monthly publications and refresh the gold tracking error mart.
    \item \textbf{Automated Warehouse Maintenance (\texttt{cpi\_maintenance\_dag}):} Scheduled weekly (Sundays at 01:00 ICT) to proactively provision monthly partitions, offload completed historical monthly partitions to compressed columnar Parquet files, run table statistics (\texttt{ANALYZE}), and curate the Metabase metadata catalog (\texttt{pipeline/metabase\_curator.py}) by hiding partition backend slices from business analysts.
\end{enumerate}
The entire stack operates locally or on secure government cloud infrastructure, providing an independent, sovereign digital public tool for Cambodia.

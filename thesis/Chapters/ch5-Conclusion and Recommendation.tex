\section{Conclusions}
\hs This graduation thesis successfully designed, implemented, and empirically validated an automated, high-frequency \textbf{Cambodia Daily Consumer Price Index (CPI) Medallion Pipeline}. The research directly solves the critical limitations of traditional manual price surveys---namely, the 30 to 60-day reporting delay, high field collection costs, and vulnerability to intra-month price shocks.

\hs By combining modern Medallion data engineering, dense multilingual text embeddings, artificial intelligence classification, axiomatic micro-econometric formulas, and machine learning nowcasting, the system achieved all four primary project objectives:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Automated Data Infrastructure and Monitoring (Objective 1):} The pipeline successfully automated 20 daily data extraction workflows across major retail supermarkets, e-commerce marketplaces, chain pharmacies, inter-provincial transit services, utility regulators, and official government data feeds. The system reliably collects over 31,000 price quotes every morning across all 12 United Nations COICOP divisions with a $99.1\%$ operational pipeline uptime, storing raw records immutably in the Bronze layer.
    \item \textbf{Exploratory Visualization of Scraped Raw Price Data (Objective 2):} The system established automated visual monitoring of raw price distributions, daily quote volumes, and category expenditure shares across all 12 consumption divisions. Automated quality validation filters out-of-stock items, detect anomalous price swings ($>3.5\sigma$), and prepare clean price observations for econometric index aggregation.
    \item \textbf{Econometric CPI Calculation and Machine Learning Inflation Estimation (Objective 3):} Implementing the axiomatic unweighted geometric mean (\textbf{Jevons formula}) at the elementary product level completely eliminated the \textbf{+1.18\% annual upward calculation drift} inherent in arithmetic formulas. Coupled with a 7-day carry-forward imputation engine and official household expenditure weights from the Cambodia Socio-Economic Survey (CSES 2023), the pipeline successfully compiles daily National Headline CPI and Refined Core CPI indicators. Machine learning models (XGBoost, LSTM, and blended ensembles) leveraging daily price momentum, fuel adjustments, and exchange rate movements achieved a \textbf{51.4\% reduction in Root Mean Squared Error} over traditional autoregressive baselines, successfully predicting monthly inflation weeks ahead of official statistical releases.
    \item \textbf{Interactive Decision-Support Dashboards (Objective 4):} The system deployed real-time executive dashboards in Metabase and Power BI, providing interactive dial gauges for headline and core inflation, drill-down charts for each of the 12 consumption divisions, top rising and falling goods, and an automated scraper health observability matrix for continuous operational monitoring.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Institutional Recommendations and Policy Roadmaps}
\hs The findings and technical assets developed in this research offer actionable strategic value for Cambodia's core macroeconomic and statistical governance institutions:

\subsection{Actionable Roadmap for the National Institute of Statistics (NIS)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hybrid Statistical Modernization:} The National Institute of Statistics should establish a formal digital data integration unit to augment physical market surveys with automated web scraping. Online data collection can immediately take over price monitoring for structured modern sectors (electronics, telecommunications, inter-provincial bus fares, and chain pharmacies), freeing up field enumerators to concentrate on rural and informal wet markets.
    \item \textbf{Adoption of Axiomatic Geometric Averaging:} In accordance with international standards \citep{imf2020cpi}, statistical compilation guidelines should mandate the Jevons geometric mean for all elementary price aggregates to prevent the formula drift documented in this research.
    \item \textbf{Public-Private Scanner Data Partnerships:} The NIS should initiate institutional agreements with major supermarket operators (such as AEON and Chip Mong) to establish automated point-of-sale scanner transaction feeds. Scanner data provides actual transaction quantities alongside prices, enabling the implementation of superlative Fisher or multilateral GEKS index systems.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Strategic Roadmap for the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High-Frequency Macroeconomic Surveillance:} The General Department of Digital Economy (GDDE) and the General Department of Policy should integrate the daily Headline and Core CPI streams into the national macroeconomic dashboard. This provides zero-delay early warning of international fuel spikes, currency pass-through shocks, and agricultural supply shortages.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Fiscal Indexation:} Public procurement contracts, infrastructure budget deflators, and civil service cost-of-living adjustments can be calibrated using real-time price trends rather than outdated historical annual estimates.
    \item \textbf{Targeted Social Safety Net Triggers:} By monitoring high-frequency price movements in basic food staples (rice, meat, and fresh fish), the government can trigger targeted cash transfers to vulnerable households before inflation erodes real household purchasing power.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Monetary Policy Roadmap for the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Exchange Rate Pass-Through Tracking:} Given Cambodia's dual-currency retail economy, the central bank can use the daily pipeline to observe exactly how quickly movements in the USD/KHR exchange rate transmit into shelf prices across different product categories.
    \item \textbf{High-Frequency Liquidity Management:} Daily inflation nowcasting provides the central bank with continuous visibility into demand pressures between monthly statistical releases, supporting timely open market operations and liquidity management.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations and Future Research Directions}
\hs While the pipeline demonstrates high reliability and predictive precision, several methodological and operational areas offer fruitful avenues for future research:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Geographic Expansion to Secondary Economic Hubs:} The current implementation focuses primarily on Phnom Penh. Future engineering work should expand automated crawlers to capture retail prices in major provincial economic centers, including Siem Reap, Battambang, Sihanoukville, and Kampong Cham.
    \item \textbf{Crowdsourced Mobile Price Collection for Wet Markets:} Informal open wet markets and roadside stalls account for a substantial share of daily food purchases in Cambodia and cannot be scraped online. Developing an offline-first mobile application that allows community enumerators or consumers to upload photographed receipts with automated text recognition would extend high-frequency price sensing into traditional retail sectors.
    \item \textbf{Alternative Satellite and Climate Regressors:} Future iterations of the machine learning nowcasting models could incorporate high-resolution satellite vegetation indices (NDVI) and rainfall anomaly data across the Mekong River basin to forecast domestic agricultural crop yields and food price movements several months in advance.
    \item \textbf{Automated Scraper Self-Healing via Language Models:} When e-commerce platforms alter their website layout, scrapers occasionally fail. Implementing autonomous artificial intelligence agents capable of inspecting modified web pages, rewriting extraction queries, and testing updated parsers automatically would further minimize maintenance overhead.
\end{enumerate}

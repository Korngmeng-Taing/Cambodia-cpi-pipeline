\chapter*{\centering ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is the primary macroeconomic benchmark for quantifying inflation and safeguarding real purchasing power, yet conventional survey methods in Cambodia face 30-to-60-day reporting delays and substantial operational costs. To address this information gap, this thesis develops the \textbf{Cambodia Daily CPI Medallion Pipeline}---an automated data engineering and nowcasting system that ingests daily price microdata at \textbf{08:00 AM ICT} across 25 retail, utility, transport, and fuel endpoints covering all 12 UN COICOP divisions in Phnom Penh.

\hs Operating on a PostgreSQL 16 Medallion architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold), the pipeline standardizes multilingual text and resolves canonical items using dense multilingual vector embeddings (\texttt{models/gemini-embedding-2}) and 7 physical attribute guardrails. Product classification into official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) 4-digit classes follows a streamlined dual architecture: single-purpose commercial outlets resolve deterministically via \textbf{Store Priority routing} ($O(1)$ SQL execution), while newly discovered multi-department supermarket items are classified in batches by \textbf{Google Gemini AI (\texttt{gemini-2.5-flash})} backed by PostgreSQL memoization (\texttt{silver.dim\_coicop\_ai\_cache}) and \textbf{domain trap guardrails} that eliminate statutory category misallocations.

\hs In the Gold layer, elementary price indices are compiled using the axiomatic unweighted geometric \textbf{Jevons formula} to eliminate the $+1.18\%$ annual upward formula drift, combined with CSES 2023 Laspeyres expenditure weights. A \textbf{Two-Stage Hybrid Ridge Nowcaster} with dynamic uncertainty decay projects real-time headline inflation, achieving an out-of-sample tracking error of just \textbf{+0.0181 percentage points} ($+0.3651\%$ projected vs. $+0.3470\%$ NIS official release). Analytical indicators are served via 3 Metabase executive dashboards and Power BI, establishing a resilient real-time inflation monitoring system for Cambodia.

\vspace{0.6cm}
\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Consumer Price Index (CPI), High-Frequency Web Scraping, Medallion Architecture, COICOP Classification, Store Priority, Gemini AI, Jevons Formula, Ridge Nowcasting.

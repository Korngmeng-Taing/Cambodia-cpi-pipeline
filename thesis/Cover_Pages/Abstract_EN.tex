\chapter*{\centering ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) serves as the primary macroeconomic indicator for measuring changes in the cost of living, calibrating monetary policy, and adjusting social safety nets. However, traditional manual monthly survey methodologies suffer from substantial temporal publication lags (30 to 60 days), intra-month volatility blind spots, and limited sampling granularity. In an economy characterized by high dollarization, strong import dependency, and extreme expenditure concentration in food and fuel ($>74\%$ of the national basket), delayed macroeconomic intelligence hinders timely policy intervention by institutions such as the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) and the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC).

\hs To overcome these structural limitations, this thesis presents the design, implementation, and empirical validation of the \textbf{Cambodia Daily Consumer Price Index (CPI) Medallion Pipeline}---an automated, production-grade big data and machine learning system for real-time inflation tracking and nowcasting. The pipeline orchestrates 25 daily automated web crawlers and administrative feeds via Apache Airflow 2.9.3, capturing high-frequency microdata across all 12 United Nations Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose (COICOP) divisions in Phnom Penh.

\hs Operating on a Medallion Star-Schema architecture within PostgreSQL 16, raw listings in the Bronze layer undergo text normalization, Khmer numeral transliteration, and metric unit conversion in the Silver layer. Longitudinal item matching combines 768-dimensional multilingual vector embeddings, multi-barcode alias mapping (\texttt{silver.canonical\_product\_barcodes}), and deterministic specification guards (pack size, memory, volume, linear dimensions, apparel size), preventing false product merges across 83,180+ canonical items. Product classification into the official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) Cambodia 4-digit COICOP Class hierarchy (\texttt{DD.G.C}, 48 classes) is governed by an AI-first ladder combining human overrides, store purity locks, rich-context Google Gemini AI inference with PostgreSQL memoization, and vetted heuristics. Across 28 historical scrape dates, all \textbf{955,789+ price observations} were 100\% classified with \textbf{0 code-division mismatches} and \textbf{0 unclassified items}, with Gemini AI powering over \textbf{53.3\%} of all classifications.

\hs In the Gold aggregation layer, micro-level daily prices are compiled using the fair unweighted geometric mean (\textbf{Jevons formula}) coupled with an ILO-standard 7-day compounded class-mean geometric imputation engine, log-linear hedonic quality adjustments for electronics, and continuous series chain-linking splice factors across annual rebasings, completely eliminating arithmetic price bounce bias. Macro-level aggregation utilizes a Modified Laspeyres formulation weighted by official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) expenditure shares to publish daily Headline CPI ($100.000\%$ basket) and Refined Core CPI ($52.200\%$ basket). Furthermore, the system deploys a \textbf{Two-Stage Hybrid Ridge Disaggregated Nowcasting Engine} combining a 9-dimensional indicator feature space, dynamic Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (\texttt{RidgeCV}), and Empirical Bayes shrinkage ($\lambda_{\text{shrink}} = \max(0, 1 - R_k^2)$) across five analytical consumption baskets. Across live production out-of-sample evaluations, the nowcaster projected headline inflation with an absolute tracking error of only \textbf{$+0.0181$ percentage points} ($+0.3651\%$ projected vs. $+0.3470\%$ NIS actual ground truth) bounded within dynamic 95\% confidence envelopes ($U_t = \sqrt{(T-t)/T}$). Supported by an automated weekly NIS portal benchmark ingestion DAG (\texttt{nis\_cpi\_dag}) and gold tracking error mart (\texttt{gold.fct\_cpi\_nis\_comparison}), the architecture effectively eliminates the 30-day official publication lag. Real-time indicators are served via 3 consolidated Metabase executive dashboards (Port 3001) and Power BI, establishing a replicable blueprint for modern digital public data infrastructure in developing Southeast Asian economies.

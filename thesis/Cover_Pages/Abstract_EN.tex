\chapter*{\centering ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is the primary macroeconomic benchmark for quantifying inflation and safeguarding real purchasing power, yet conventional survey methods in Cambodia face 30-to-60-day reporting delays and substantial operational costs. To address this information gap, this thesis develops the \textbf{Cambodia Daily CPI Medallion Pipeline}---an automated data engineering and nowcasting system that ingests daily price microdata at \textbf{08:00 AM ICT} across 25 retail, utility, transport, and fuel endpoints covering all 12 UN COICOP divisions in Phnom Penh.

\hs Operating on a PostgreSQL 16 Medallion architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold), the pipeline standardizes multilingual text and resolves canonical items using dense multilingual vector embeddings and physical attribute guardrails. Product classification into official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) 4-digit classes follows a streamlined dual architecture: single-purpose commercial outlets resolve deterministically via \textbf{Store Priority routing} with zero latency, while newly discovered multi-department supermarket items are classified in batches by \textbf{Google Gemini AI} backed by relational database memoization and \textbf{domain trap guardrails} that eliminate statutory category misallocations.

\hs In the Gold layer, elementary price indices are compiled using a \textbf{Two-Stage Store-Balanced Jevons formula} across an \textbf{irregular COICOP hierarchy} (76 leaf Elementary Aggregates: 41 direct classes and 35 detailed subclasses) to eliminate the $+1.18\%$ annual upward formula drift and prevent large-catalog supermarket dominance ($-0.28$ index point bias). Combining multi-tier expenditure aggregation with official CSES 2023 weights, the system was validated against the official August 2026 National Institute of Statistics (NIS) benchmark, achieving an MAE of just \textbf{0.12 index points (0.055\% relative difference)}. A \textbf{Two-Stage Hybrid Ridge Nowcaster} with dynamic uncertainty decay projects real-time headline inflation, achieving an out-of-sample tracking error of just \textbf{+0.0181 percentage points} ($+0.3651\%$ projected vs. $+0.3470\%$ NIS official release). Analytical indicators are served via 3 Metabase executive dashboards and Power BI, establishing a resilient real-time inflation monitoring system for Cambodia.

\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Consumer Price Index (CPI), High-Frequency Web Scraping, Medallion Architecture, COICOP Classification, Store Priority, Gemini AI, Jevons Formula, Ridge Nowcasting.

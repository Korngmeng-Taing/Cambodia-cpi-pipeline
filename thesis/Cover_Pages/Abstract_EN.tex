\chapter*{\centering ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

Monitoring consumer price dynamics with high temporal frequency and structural precision is essential for macroeconomic stability, monetary policy calibration, and social welfare protection in Cambodia. The National Institute of Statistics (NIS) currently compiles the national Consumer Price Index (CPI) through monthly manual field surveys across physical wet markets and retail establishments. While methodologically established, this traditional approach suffers from an inherent 30 to 60-day publication lag, intra-month price volatility blind spots, and vulnerability to unobserved shrinkflation and product churn.

This graduation thesis addresses these fundamental challenges by designing, implementing, and deploying an automated, end-to-end \textbf{Cambodia Daily Consumer Price Index (CPI) Medallion Pipeline} on a production-grade data engineering infrastructure. The system executes four integrated contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Multi-Source Bronze Ingestion:} Automated orchestration of 20 daily scrapers via Apache Airflow 2.9.3 across modern supermarkets (AEON 1, AEON 3, DeliShop), digital pharmacies (Community Pharmacy), transport aggregators (redBus, BookMeBus), telecom providers (Cellcard, Smart), and official administrative APIs (Ministry of Commerce retail fuel tariffs, Ministry of Economy and Finance USD/KHR exchange rates), capturing over 16,000 price quotes daily with 99.5\% system availability.
    \item \textbf{Semantic Entity Resolution with Deterministic Specification Guards:} A hybrid item matching engine utilizing 768-dimensional dense vector embeddings (\texttt{text-embedding-004}) combined with strict deterministic physical specification guards (RAM/storage capacity, volume tolerance $\le 10\%$, and pack-multiplier equality) and Large Language Model (Gemini Flash) arbitration for borderline pairs, achieving an \textbf{F1-score of 95.6\%} and reducing false merge rates from 10.9\% to \textbf{2.2\%}.
    \item \textbf{4-Tier Hybrid AI UN COICOP Classifier:} A hierarchical classification engine that resolves 93.4\% of all items in sub-millisecond time through human authority overrides, 15 single-category store domain locks, and 12-division reference vector cosine matching, with Gemini Pro/Flash fallback memoized in PostgreSQL, attaining an overall accuracy of \textbf{98.2\%} across all 12 UN COICOP divisions.
    \item \textbf{Econometric Index Compilation and Operational Serving:} Implementation of unweighted geometric mean price relatives (\textbf{Jevons formula}) coupled with a \textbf{7-day ILO class-mean missing price imputation engine} that reduces synthetic basket variance by \textbf{64.2\%}, aggregated under official NIS expenditure weights into daily National Headline CPI and Refined Core CPI (52.200\% non-food/non-fuel basket) published live on Metabase v0.49 and Power BI.
\end{enumerate}

The empirical findings prove that high-frequency web scraping combined with dense vector representations and axiomatic index formulas can effectively eliminate retrospective information lag, providing central banks and policy institutions with zero-lag inflation intelligence while maintaining strict compliance with international statistical standards.

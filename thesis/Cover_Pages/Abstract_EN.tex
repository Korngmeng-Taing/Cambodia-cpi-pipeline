\chapter*{\centering ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

\hs The Consumer Price Index (CPI) serves as the primary macroeconomic indicator for measuring changes in the cost of living, calibrating monetary policy, and adjusting social safety nets. However, traditional manual monthly survey methodologies suffer from substantial temporal publication lags (30 to 60 days), intra-month volatility blind spots, and limited sampling granularity. In an economy characterized by high dollarization, strong import dependency, and extreme expenditure concentration in food and fuel ($>74\%$ of the national basket), delayed macroeconomic intelligence hinders timely policy intervention by institutions such as the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) and the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC).

\hs To overcome these structural limitations, this thesis presents the design, implementation, and empirical validation of the \textbf{Cambodia Daily Consumer Price Index (CPI) Medallion Pipeline}---an automated, production-grade big data and machine learning system for real-time inflation tracking and nowcasting. The pipeline orchestrates 23 daily automated web crawlers and administrative feeds via Apache Airflow 2.9.3, capturing high-frequency microdata across all 12 United Nations Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose (COICOP) divisions in Phnom Penh.

\hs Operating on a Medallion Star-Schema architecture within PostgreSQL 16, raw JSONB records in the Bronze layer undergo text normalization, Khmer numeral transliteration, and metric unit conversion in the Silver layer. Longitudinal entity resolution is achieved via a hybrid engine combining 768-dimensional dense vector embeddings (\texttt{text-embedding-004}) with deterministic specification guards, achieving high semantic precision across noisy retail titles. Product classification into the 12 COICOP divisions is governed by a 4-tier ladder combining human overrides, store domain locks, vector centroid matching, and batched Google Gemini LLM inference with PostgreSQL caching.

\hs In the Gold aggregation layer, micro-level daily prices are compiled using the axiomatic unweighted geometric mean (\textbf{Jevons formula}) coupled with an international ILO-standard 7-day compounded class-mean geometric imputation engine, log-linear hedonic quality adjustments for electronics, and continuous series chain-linking splice factors across annual rebasings, completely eliminating arithmetic price bounce bias. Macro-level aggregation utilizes a Modified Laspeyres formulation weighted by official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) expenditure shares to publish daily Headline CPI ($100.000\%$ basket) and Refined Core CPI ($52.200\%$ basket). Furthermore, the system deploys an expanding-window intra-month inflation nowcasting engine that projects unobserved calendar days using leading food and transport momentum signals and chain-links the estimated monthly growth rate to official National Institute of Statistics (NIS) benchmarks with dynamic 95\% confidence intervals, effectively eliminating the 30-day official publication lag. Real-time indicators are served via containerized Metabase and Power BI executive dashboards, establishing a replicable blueprint for modern digital public data infrastructure in developing Southeast Asian economies.
